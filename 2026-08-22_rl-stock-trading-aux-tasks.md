# Self-Supervised Auxiliary Task Discovery for Stable RL Stock Trading
**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Keyword:** stock trading
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15841

## Summary
Introduces self-supervised auxiliary tasks that stabilize reinforcement learning for stock trading. The method discovers useful auxiliary objectives automatically, improving sample efficiency and reducing policy collapse during training.

## Idea angle
Apply the auxiliary task discovery method to IDX trading — Indonesian market data is limited, so auxiliary tasks that improve sample efficiency are especially valuable. Could also extend to crypto trading on local exchanges.

## Details
Authors: Arishi Orra, Himanshu Choudhary, Manoj Thakur. Published Aug 2026. Cross-listed from cs.LG. Subjects: Machine Learning, Computational Finance.
