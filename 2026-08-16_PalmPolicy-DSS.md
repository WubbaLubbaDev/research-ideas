## Summary
PalmPolicy-DSS is an AI-driven Decision Support System designed to help policymakers analyze, simulate, and optimize palm oil tariff policies. It combines economic modeling with AI to evaluate the impact of different tariff structures on trade flows, domestic prices, and supply chain dynamics.

## Idea angle
Adapt this DSS for Indonesian palm oil policy — Indonesia is the world's largest CPO producer, and tariff/export duty decisions directly impact millions of smallholders. The gap: Indonesian policymakers lack accessible tools to simulate the downstream effects of CPO export duty changes on smallholder income and domestic cooking oil prices.
