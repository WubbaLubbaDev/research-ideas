# Strat-LLM: Stratified Strategy Alignment for LLM-based Stock Trading
**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Keyword:** stock trading
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06024

## Summary
A framework for LLM-based trading that introduces stratified strategy alignment — Free, Guided, and Strict modes — operating in a live-forward setting across A-share and US markets with real-time prices, news, and annual reports.

## Idea angle
The three-mode alignment (Free/Guided/Strict) is a practical contribution — it lets traders choose how much autonomy to give an LLM based on market conditions. The finding that mid-scale models (35B) outperform ultra-large models (122B) under strict constraints suggests an opportunity for efficient on-device trading agents.

## Details
Authors: Wenliang Huang, Zengyi Yu | Submitted: May 2026 | Accepted at IJCNN 2026 | Key finding: standard LLMs fall into a 'high win-rate trap' — small gains at the expense of total returns.
