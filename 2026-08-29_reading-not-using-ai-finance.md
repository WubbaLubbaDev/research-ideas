# Reading Is Not Using: Retrieval, Judgment, and AI Financial Research Workflows
**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24842v1

## Summary
A paper examining the gap between LLMs reading financial disclosures and actually using them for investment decisions. Most AI analyst evaluations measure reading comprehension, but the real challenge is judgment — turning read information into actionable decisions. Published Aug 25, 2026.

## Idea angle
The reading-vs-using distinction is critical for AI in finance. A model that can summarize a 10-K perfectly may still make terrible investment decisions. The paper's insight — that evaluation must measure judgment, not just comprehension — should reshape how we benchmark AI financial tools.
