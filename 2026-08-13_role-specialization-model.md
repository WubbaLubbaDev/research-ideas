# Role Specialization Model: Coordinating LLM Tools in Agentic Software Development
**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Keyword:** AI agent
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12311v1

## Summary
This paper presents a case study on the Role Specialization Model (RSM) for coordinating LLM-based tools in agentic software development (SE 3.0), where autonomous agents manage full development life cycles under human supervision.

## Idea angle
Implement RSM in a multi-agent development team for Indonesian outsourcing companies — agents specialized as frontend, backend, QA, and DevOps engineers coordinated by a human supervisor. This could help Indonesian IT outsourcing firms scale delivery capacity without proportional hiring, addressing the severe developer shortage in Indonesia.

Authors: Carlos Alberto Fernández-y-Fernández, Jorge R. Aguilar-Cisneros. arXiv: 2608.12311.
