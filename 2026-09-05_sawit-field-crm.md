## Summary
A field CRM operational app for palm oil enumerators, built with Streamlit and Lark Bitable API. Handles field data collection, smallholder registration, and operational workflows for palm oil supply chain management.

## Idea angle
Field CRM for palm oil enumerators directly addresses the smallholder data collection challenge. The Lark Bitable integration is clever — using a consumer-friendly spreadsheet API for field data in low-connectivity environments. Extending this with offline sync and GPS-verified plot boundaries would make it production-ready for Indonesian palm oil cooperatives.
