## Summary
Automated stock trading bot combining technical analysis, machine learning, and risk management in Python. Integrates multiple signal sources for buy/sell decision-making.

## Idea angle
Combining ML with technical analysis in a bot framework is the standard approach, but the risk management integration is what differentiates sustainable traders from gamblers. Open-sourcing the risk management module separately — position sizing, drawdown limits, correlation-aware allocation — would be more valuable than yet another signal generator.
