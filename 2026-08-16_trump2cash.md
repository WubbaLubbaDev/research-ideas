## Summary
A trading bot that detects mentions of publicly traded companies in political figures' social media posts and automatically executes trades based on sentiment analysis. It demonstrated how quickly market-moving information spreads through social media.

## Idea angle
Build a similar bot for Indonesian political figures' social media — Jokowi, Prabowo, Luhut, and other ministers whose statements move IDX stocks (especially mining, palm oil, infrastructure). The gap: Indonesian political statements significantly impact specific stocks but no automated system captures this signal. NLP models tuned for Bahasa Indonesia would be essential.
