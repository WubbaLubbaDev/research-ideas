# Machine Learning and Deep Learning in Computational Finance: A Systematic Review
**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.21588

## Summary
A systematic review (PRISMA 2020) analyzing 22 peer-reviewed articles (2024-2026) on ML and DL applications in computational finance, covering credit risk prediction, cryptocurrency, asset pricing, and macroeconomic policy modeling.

## Idea angle
Identifies that XGBoost, LSTM, and hybrid models dominate, while explainable AI (SHAP) is emerging for transparency. A gap exists in generalizability across market regimes — an opportunity for regime-aware ensemble models that adapt model selection dynamically.

## Details
Authors: Soufiane El Amine El Alami et al. | Submitted: Nov 2025 | Cited by 18 | Key finding: ML/DL outperform traditional models but interpretability and data quality remain challenges.
