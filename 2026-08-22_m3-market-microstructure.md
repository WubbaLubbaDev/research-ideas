# M3: State-Event Generative Foundation Model for Market Microstructure
**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19227

## Summary
M3 is a generative foundation model that captures market microstructure dynamics using state-event representations. It models order flow and price impact at the microstructure level, enabling realistic market simulation.

## Idea angle
Use M3 to generate synthetic market data for emerging exchanges like IDX where historical tick data is scarce or expensive. Train trading agents on simulated microstructure rather than relying on limited real data.

## Details
Authors: Yanzhi Zhang, Yu Ma, Yilin Cheng, Jian Li, Yitong Duan. Published Aug 2026. Subjects: Computational Finance, Machine Learning.
