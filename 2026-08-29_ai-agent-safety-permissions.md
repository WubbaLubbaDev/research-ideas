# AI Agent Safety Is Still Thinking Like Permissions
**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Keyword:** AI agent
**Source:** Hacker News
**URL:** https://jeffreyflynt02.medium.com/ai-agent-safety-is-still-thinking-like-permissions-45878337bd98

## Summary
An HN story arguing that current AI agent safety approaches are stuck in a permissions mindset — granting or denying access to tools — when the real challenge is reasoning about the consequences of actions. Posted Aug 2026.

## Idea angle
The permissions paradigm is necessary but insufficient. An agent with 'write' permission to a database could still cause harm by writing the wrong data. Safety needs to evolve from 'can you do this?' to 'should you do this?' — consequence-aware decision-making, not just access control. This is especially critical for trading agents where 'can trade' ≠ 'should trade this position now'.
