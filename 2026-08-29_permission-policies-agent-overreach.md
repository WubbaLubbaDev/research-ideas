# User-Authored Permission Policies for AI Agent Overreach Protection
**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Keyword:** AI agent
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27443v1

## Summary
A paper investigating whether user-authored permission policies — written by non-programmers — can effectively protect against AI agent overreach. Studies the trade-off between understandability and protection strength. Published Aug 27, 2026.

## Idea angle
Permission policies are the access control layer for agents. If non-technical users can write effective policies, agent safety becomes democratized. If not, we need policy templates and wizards. The paper's findings should directly inform the design of agent permission UIs — including trading agent risk controls.
