# AgriChat — Multimodal LLM for Agriculture Image Understanding
**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Keyword:** palm oil
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16934

## Summary
AgriChat is a multimodal LLM specifically designed for agriculture image understanding. It can analyze crop photos, identify diseases, recommend treatments, and answer agricultural questions in natural language.

## Idea angle
Fine-tune AgriChat for oil palm specifically — Indonesian palm oil smallholders need a tool where they can photograph their trees and get instant diagnosis and treatment advice in Bahasa Indonesia. No such tool exists.
