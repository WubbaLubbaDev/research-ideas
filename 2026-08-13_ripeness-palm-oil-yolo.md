## Summary
A ripeness detection app for palm oil fruit using custom-trained YOLOv5 models, classifying fruit ripeness from images to support harvest optimization decisions.

## Idea angle
Deploy this as a mobile app for Indonesian smallholder farmers — most smallholders determine harvest readiness by visual inspection alone, leading to suboptimal timing. An AI-powered ripeness checker running on a smartphone could improve yield by 10-15% by ensuring harvest at optimal ripeness. Integrate with SawitPRO's existing smallholder platform.
