## Summary
A plantation-to-refinery traceability system for the world's largest palm oil producer, using Snowflake Dynamic Tables to build chain-of-custody across 300 mills, Iceberg for auditor self-service via Athena, and Row Access Policies for trader-level data segregation.

## Idea angle
The multi-mill traceability architecture is directly relevant to Indonesian palm oil supply chain tech. Could adapt this pattern for smallholder cooperatives — tracking FFB from smallholder plots to mill, with SMS-based reporting for farmers who lack smartphones.
