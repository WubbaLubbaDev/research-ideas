# Toward Decentralized Carbon Trading in Indonesia via Public Blockchain
**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Keyword:** palm oil
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15597

## Summary
Proposes a public blockchain architecture for tokenized real-world carbon assets in Indonesia. Enables decentralized carbon trading where carbon credits from palm oil sustainability projects can be tokenized and traded transparently.

## Idea angle
Build a carbon credit marketplace for Indonesian palm oil plantations — tokenize RSPO certification credits and carbon sequestration from replanting. The blockchain approach addresses trust issues in Indonesia's voluntary carbon market.

## Details
Authors: Rischan Mafrur, Fadli Ikhsan Pratama, Khadijah. Published Aug 2026. Indonesia-specific, blockchain, tokenized RWA.
