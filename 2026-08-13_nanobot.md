**Date:** 2026-08-13

## Summary
Nanobot is a self-hosted multi-agent AI assistant with MCP (Model Context Protocol) support, Discord/Telegram bot integration, workflow automation, and an OpenAI-compatible API. It's designed for personal AI infrastructure.

## Idea angle
Deploy Nanobot as a self-hosted personal AI assistant for Indonesian small businesses — handling customer service via WhatsApp, managing inventory queries, and automating routine tasks. Self-hosting addresses data sovereignty concerns that Indonesian businesses have with US-based AI services, and the MCP ecosystem enables integration with local tools.
