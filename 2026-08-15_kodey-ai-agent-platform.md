## Summary
Kodey.ai is an AI agent platform where you describe what you want and the agent forms accordingly — behavior shaped by your words, with data and tools woven into the agent's capabilities.

## Idea angle
The 'describe-to-build' approach lowers the barrier for agent creation. Could adapt this pattern to financial agents — 'build me an agent that monitors Indonesian tech stocks and alerts me when RSI crosses 70' — with pre-built financial tool integrations and strategy templates.

## Details
Product Hunt listing. 220 followers. Positions itself as building agents that 'work' vs just 'chat'.
