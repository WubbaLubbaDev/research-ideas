## Summary
Python project for data collection, quantitative analytics, and technical structure analysis across eight financial markets. Combines multi-market data pipelines with AI-driven analysis.

## Idea angle
Multi-market quant analysis is typically siloed per asset class. A unified pipeline across 8 markets (equities, forex, crypto, commodities, etc.) could expose cross-market correlation signals that single-market tools miss — especially useful for traders in emerging markets who navigate multiple asset classes simultaneously.
