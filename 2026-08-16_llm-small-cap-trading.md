# LLM-Driven Small-Capitalization Trading: Financial News Sentiment + Macro + Technical Signals
**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12283v1

## Summary
This paper explores using LLMs to extract richer signals from financial news than fixed sentiment lexicons, feeding them into portfolio construction for small-cap stocks. It introduces uncertainty-aware portfolio optimization that decomposes model-predicted risk into aleatoric and epistemic components.

## Idea angle
Apply this LLM-driven approach to Indonesian small-cap stocks on IDX, where news coverage is sparse and sentiment is harder to capture with traditional lexicons. The gap: Indonesian small-cap stocks are under-researched; an LLM that reads Indonesian-language financial news (Bisnis, Kontan, Detik Finance) and extracts trading signals could surface alpha in overlooked names.

## Details
- Authors: Alireza Kargarzadeh, Nariman Khaledian, Navid Parvini
- Key concepts: uncertainty-aware portfolio construction, aleatoric/epistemic risk decomposition
- Combines LLM sentiment with macro indicators and technical signals
