## Summary
PortfolioU implements a Fuzzy Moving Average System with Genetic Algorithm optimization specifically for trading Crude Palm Oil (CPO) futures. It combines fuzzy logic for trend identification with GA-optimized parameters for entry/exit rules, targeting the Malaysian CPO futures market.

## Idea angle
Adapt this CPO futures trading system for the Indonesian derivatives market (BKDI, Bursa Berjangka Jakarta). The gap: Indonesian CPO traders lack specialized algorithmic tools for CPO futures. Since Indonesia and Malaysia CPO prices are highly correlated but not identical, re-optimizing the GA parameters for Indonesian CPO contracts could yield different alpha signals.
