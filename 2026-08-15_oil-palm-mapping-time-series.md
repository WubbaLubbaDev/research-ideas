# From Noisy Historical Maps to Time-Series Oil Palm Mapping Without Annotation
**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Keyword:** palm oil
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/2604.23776v1

## Summary
A deep learning framework generating 10-meter resolution oil palm plantation maps for Indonesia and Malaysia (2020-2024) using Sentinel-2 imagery without requiring new manual annotations. Uses U-Net with Determinant-based Mutual Information to handle noisy 100m historical labels.

## Idea angle
The annotation-free mapping approach is scalable — no need for expensive ground truth labeling. Could use these maps to build a real-time plantation expansion monitoring tool that alerts sustainability auditors when new land is converted to palm oil, cross-referenced with EUDR compliance databases.

## Details
Authors: Nuttaset Kuapanich et al. (Sun Yat-Sen University) | Submitted: Apr 2026 | Validated against 2,058 points. Oil palm coverage peaked in 2022, declined in 2024. Datasets publicly available on Zenodo.
