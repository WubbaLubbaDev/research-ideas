# Self-Supervised Auxiliary Task Discovery for Stable RL in Stock Trading
**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Keyword:** stock trading
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15841v1

## Summary
A paper on using self-supervised auxiliary task discovery to stabilize reinforcement learning for stock trading. RL trading policies often suffer from instability due to non-stationary markets. The approach discovers helpful auxiliary tasks that improve learning stability. Published Aug 16, 2026.

## Idea angle
Stability is the #1 problem in RL-based trading — a policy that works in training collapses in live markets. Self-supervised auxiliary tasks that force the agent to learn market structure (not just optimize reward) could bridge the train-test gap. This approach could be combined with LLM agents for hybrid neuro-symbolic trading.
