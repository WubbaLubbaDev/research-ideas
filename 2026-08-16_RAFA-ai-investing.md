## Summary
RAFA uses AI agents to help users invest like experts with personalized insights. Each agent specializes in one task — fundamental analysis, technical analysis, risk management — functioning as a team of specialized experts working together to provide investment recommendations.

## Idea angle
Build a multi-agent investment analysis tool for Indonesian retail investors where each agent covers a different aspect of IDX stock analysis. The gap: Indonesian retail investors lack access to institutional-grade multi-perspective analysis. Specialized agents could cover: IDX sector fundamentals, commodity linkages (CPO, coal), rupiah impact, and political risk factors.
