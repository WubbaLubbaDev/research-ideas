# Sectoral Inter-Dependencies Drive Loss of Structural Balance in Financial Networks
**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12023v1

## Summary
This paper models financial markets as signed graphs showing cooperation and conflict between sectors, analyzing how sectoral inter-dependencies drive the loss of structural balance in financial networks.

## Idea angle
Map IDX sector inter-dependencies as a signed network — Indonesian sectors have unique relationships (banking↔commodities↔property) that differ from US/EU structures. Understanding these sectoral linkages could improve portfolio diversification advice for Indonesian investors who tend to over-concentrate in banking and property stocks.

## Details
Authors: Kartik Dahake, Abhijit Chakraborty. arXiv: 2608.12023.
