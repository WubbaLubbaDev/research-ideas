# The European Union Deforestation Regulation: The Impact on Argentina
**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Keyword:** palm oil
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.11796

## Summary
An analysis of the economic and environmental impacts of the EUDR on Argentina's exports, estimating that only 2.84% of covered exports are non-compliant. Simulations show limited GDP impact (0.14%) but significant environmental benefits (2.45% reduction in deforested hectares).

## Idea angle
The EUDR's impact varies by country and commodity — for palm oil exporters like Indonesia, the compliance burden is much higher. Could build an EUDR compliance cost calculator specifically for Indonesian palm oil producers that estimates certification costs, traceability system costs, and potential market access impacts.
