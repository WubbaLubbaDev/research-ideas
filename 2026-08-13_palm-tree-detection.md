# PalmTreeDetection: AI computer vision for harvest route optimization
**Date:** 2026-08-13

**Source:** GitHub trending

## Summary
An AI computer vision model for enhancing and optimizing harvest routes in palm oil plantations and monitoring tree health, using aerial imagery to detect individual palm trees and assess their condition.

## Idea angle
Combine palm tree detection with route optimization to create harvest planning software for Indonesian plantations. Large plantations have thousands of trees — knowing which blocks are ready for harvest and optimizing harvester routes could reduce labor costs significantly. This directly serves SawitPRO's use case of digitizing the harvest workflow.
