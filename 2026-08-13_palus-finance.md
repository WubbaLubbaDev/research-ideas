# Palus Finance (YC W26): Better yields on idle startup cash
**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** Hacker News
**URL:** https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47278980

## Summary
Palus Finance is a YC W26 startup building a treasury management platform for startups and SMBs to earn higher yields on idle cash. It automates cash deployment across money market funds and short-duration instruments.

## Idea angle
Build a similar treasury yield optimizer for Indonesian SMEs, where idle cash often sits in low-interest bank accounts. Indonesian money market instruments (SBN, depo) have higher yields than US equivalents — a product that auto-deploys to the best rates could capture significant value in a market with rising rates.
