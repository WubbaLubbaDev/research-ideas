# Auditable by Construction: Ontology-Driven Framework for Trustworthy LLM Analytics in Enterprise Finance
**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20661v1

## Summary
A paper proposing an ontology-driven framework for trustworthy LLM analytics in enterprise finance — specifically Financial Planning and Analysis (FP&A). The framework makes LLM outputs auditable by construction, not after the fact. Published Aug 21, 2026.

## Idea angle
Audit-by-construction is the right approach for regulated finance — you can't bolt on auditability after the fact. The ontology-driven approach means every LLM output is traceable to its reasoning chain and data sources. This is essential for any enterprise deploying LLMs in FP&A, reporting, or compliance workflows.
