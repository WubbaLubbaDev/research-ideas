# The 2025 AI Agent Index: Documenting Technical and Safety Features
**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Keyword:** AI agent
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17753

## Summary
A comprehensive index documenting the origins, design, capabilities, ecosystem, and safety features of 30 state-of-the-art AI agents. Finds different transparency levels among developers, with most sharing little information about safety, evaluations, and societal impacts.

## Idea angle
The lack of safety transparency is a market failure — developers self-report selectively. Could build an independent agent safety rating system that red-teams popular agent frameworks and publishes standardized safety scores, similar to crash test ratings for cars.

## Details
Authors: Leon Staufer et al. | Accepted at ACM FAccT 2026 | 30 agents documented | Available at aiagentindex.mit.edu
