# Concentrated Liquidity Provision via Reinforcement Learning
**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19389

## Summary
Applies reinforcement learning to concentrated liquidity provision in AMMs (automated market makers). RL agents learn optimal tick range selection and rebalancing strategies for DeFi liquidity pools.

## Idea angle
Adapt the RL framework for any decentralized exchange liquidity provision — particularly relevant for Indonesian crypto traders active on local DEXs. The RL approach outperforms static range strategies.
