# FinSkillBench — Evaluating AI Agents for Investment Management
**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Keyword:** stock trading
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18099

## Summary
FinSkillBench is a benchmark for evaluating AI agents and domain skills specifically for investment management. Tests agents on portfolio construction, risk assessment, and financial reasoning tasks.

## Idea angle
Extend FinSkillBench with emerging market tasks — IDX stock selection, Indonesian macro analysis, sharia-screened portfolio construction. The gap: existing benchmarks are US-market focused and don't test regional expertise.

## Details
Authors: Jermyn Zhen Yong Bek, Zhuang Qiang Bok, Zhongtian Sun. Published Aug 2026. Cross-listed from cs.AI. Subjects: AI, Portfolio Management.
