# From Financial Sentiment to Return Predictability — QLoRA Benchmark
**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Keyword:** stock trading
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04200

## Summary
Benchmarks QLoRA-finetuned LLMs on financial sentiment classification and tests whether sentiment predictions translate to actual return predictability. Finds that good sentiment classification doesn't always mean profitable trading signals.

## Idea angle
Replicate the benchmark for Indonesian market sentiment — use Bahasa Indonesia financial news and social media. The gap: no sentiment-return study exists for IDX stocks, and Bahasa Indonesia NLP for finance is underdeveloped.

## Details
Author: Fusheng Luo. Published Aug 2026. Subjects: Mathematical Finance, Machine Learning. Submitted to ICAIF.
