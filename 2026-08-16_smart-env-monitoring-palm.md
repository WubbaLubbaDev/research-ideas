## Summary
An IoT-based environmental monitoring system for palm oil plantations that remotely tracks critical environmental factors essential for crop health — soil moisture, temperature, humidity, rainfall. It provides real-time data to plantation managers for better decision-making.

## Idea angle
Deploy a similar IoT monitoring system for Indonesian smallholder plots that currently lack any environmental monitoring. The gap: smallholders rely on intuition rather than data for irrigation and fertilization decisions. Low-cost IoT sensors connected to a mobile app could bridge this gap, especially when combined with yield models from PySawit.
