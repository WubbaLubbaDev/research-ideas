# AVA-Encoder: Agent-Native Video Representation Learning
**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Keyword:** ai-agent
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12313v1

## Summary
AVA-Encoder addresses the gap between film content and agent-usable representations, creating a structured video representation that is both faithful to content and directly usable for agentic reasoning and manipulation. It enables creative agents to learn from high-quality human films.

## Idea angle
Apply agent-native video representations to Indonesian content moderation — agents that can understand video context (not just frame-by-frame classification) would improve moderation of user-generated content on Indonesian platforms (TikTok Indonesia, YouTube). The gap: contextual video understanding for Indonesian-language content is underserved.

## Details
- Authors: Chuyue Li, Jinpeng Yu, Haozhe Wang
- Key concept: structured video representation for agentic reasoning
- Application: creative agents learning from cinematic content
