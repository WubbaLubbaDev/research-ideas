# Time-Series Oil Palm Mapping Without Annotation in Malaysia and Indonesia
**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Keyword:** palm oil
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23776v1

## Summary
A paper on mapping oil palm plantations across Malaysia and Indonesia (2020-2024) using noisy historical maps without fresh annotations. Addresses the critical challenge of tracking plantation expansion for environmental conservation. Published April 26, 2026.

## Idea angle
Annotation-free mapping is a breakthrough for scaling — you don't need experts to label every satellite image. For Indonesia's EUDR compliance, time-series plantation maps are essential to prove deforestation-free supply chains. This approach could be operationalized as a monitoring service for palm oil supply chain transparency.
