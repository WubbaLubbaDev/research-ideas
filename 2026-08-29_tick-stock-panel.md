## Summary
A 3.9K-star self-hosted, zero-ops A-share (Chinese stock) quant workbench with stock screening, monitoring, and backtesting. Uses LLM capabilities for strategy customization, individual stock analysis, and market review. Built with React, FastAPI, and DuckDB.

## Idea angle
The 'self-hosted, zero-ops' angle is important — retail quant traders want power without infrastructure burden. The LLM-driven strategy customization is the differentiator vs. traditional quant platforms. This architecture (React + FastAPI + DuckDB + LLM) is a clean template for building similar tools for other emerging markets.
