## Summary
TradingView is the dominant charting and social trading platform for crypto, stocks, and forex, offering real-time charts, indicators, and a community of traders sharing ideas and strategies.

## Idea angle
TradingView supports IDX data but the local community is thin. Building a TradingView-integrated Indonesian trading community — with localized content, IDX-specific indicators (foreign ownership ratio, auto-rejection probability), and social features — on top of TradingView's API could aggregate the fragmented Indonesian trading community.
