## Summary
Kai is an AI-powered trading wizard that provides instant chart analysis and trade setups with one click. It automates technical analysis — reading chart patterns, identifying support/resistance, and generating actionable trade recommendations for retail traders.

## Idea angle
Build an AI chart analysis tool specifically for IDX stocks with Indonesian-language support. The gap: Indonesian retail traders rely on manual chart analysis or English-language tools. An AI that reads IDX charts, identifies patterns relevant to Indonesian market behavior, and explains them in Bahasa Indonesia would serve the fast-growing local trading community.
