# Amigo AI: Private AI-Powered Finance Hub
**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** Product Hunt
**URL:** https://www.producthunt.com/products/amigo-ai-going-deeper-in-transactions

## Summary
Amigo AI is a privacy-focused personal finance hub that manages multiple accounts, budgets, goals, debts, assets, and recurring expenses. Its web beta emphasizes local data ownership and comprehensive financial management in one place.

## Idea angle
Build a privacy-first finance hub for Indonesian users who distrust cloud-based finance apps. The gap: Indonesian consumers are increasingly privacy-conscious after several data breach scandals; a locally-hosted, encrypted personal finance tool would differentiate. Could integrate with Maybe Finance's open-source foundation.

## Details
- Features: multi-account management, budgeting, debt tracking, asset management
- Focus: privacy-first, private AI-powered
- Status: Web beta
