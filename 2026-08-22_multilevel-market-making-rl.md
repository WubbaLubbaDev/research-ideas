# Multi-Level Market Making with Reinforcement Learning
**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Keyword:** stock trading
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18195

## Summary
Applies RL to multi-level market making — agents learn to quote bids and asks at multiple price levels simultaneously. Extends single-level market making to realistic multi-level order book dynamics.

## Idea angle
Adapt for crypto market making on Indonesian exchanges — the local crypto market has less sophisticated market makers, so an RL-based approach could capture more spread. Build with realistic fee structures and latency models.

## Details
Authors: Patrick Cheridito, Moritz Weiss. Published Aug 2026. Subjects: Trading and Market Microstructure.
