# From Knowing to Doing: Memory-Controlled Benchmark for LLM Trading Agents
**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Keyword:** stock trading
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28359v1

## Summary
A benchmark paper evaluating whether LLM agents can profit in capital markets, with a focus on memory — can agents learn from past trades and market conditions? The benchmark goes beyond end-to-end trading to test memory-controlled decision-making. Published May 27, 2026.

## Idea angle
Memory is the underexplored dimension in LLM trading agents. Most benchmarks test one-shot decisions; this one tests whether the agent improves over time. The 'knowing vs. doing' framing captures the gap between financial knowledge (which LLMs have) and trading skill (which they often lack). Memory could be the bridge.
