**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Keyword:** stock trading
**Source:** Hacker News

## Summary
Bloomberg reports that 24-hour stock trading is booming as retail platforms extend hours, challenging traditional market structures. Wall Street firms are concerned about liquidity and risk management outside normal hours.

## Idea angle
IDX already has extended trading hours but lacks the infrastructure for truly 24-hour trading. As crypto and global markets push toward 24/7 availability, Indonesian exchanges will face pressure to extend hours. Building the risk management and circuit breaker infrastructure for extended-hours IDX trading could be a valuable B2B product for IDX/ICDX.
