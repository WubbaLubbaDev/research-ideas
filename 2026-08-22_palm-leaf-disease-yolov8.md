## Summary
Uses YOLOv8 object detection to identify palm oil leaf diseases from images. Enables early detection of common diseases like Ganoderma basal stem rot by analyzing leaf symptoms visible in field photos.

## Idea angle
Deploy as a mobile app for Indonesian smallholders — most palm oil farmers have smartphones but no access to agronomists. A photo-based disease detection app with treatment recommendations could save significant yield losses.
