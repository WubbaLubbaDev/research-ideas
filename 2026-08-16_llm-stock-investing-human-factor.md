# Large Language Models and Stock Investing: Is the Human Factor Required?
**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Keyword:** stock-trading
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19944v2

## Summary
This paper evaluates whether LLMs (ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Perplexity) can generate reliable stock market predictions across three prompting strategies: naive, structured, and chain-of-thought. It asks whether human judgment is still necessary when LLMs provide investment recommendations.

## Idea angle
Replicate this study for Indonesian stocks using local LLMs or multilingual models. The gap: no study has evaluated LLM stock-picking quality on emerging markets like IDX. Testing whether models trained primarily on US market data can reason about Indonesian companies would reveal both model limitations and localization opportunities.

## Details
- Models evaluated: ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Perplexity
- Strategies: naive query, structured approach, chain-of-thought reasoning
- Key question: can LLMs replace human stock analysis?
