## Summary
A 3D digital twin simulation of palm oil mill processes with computer vision integration. Simulates the milling workflow and uses CV to monitor quality at various stages of palm oil processing.

## Idea angle
Build a full digital twin for Indonesian palm oil mills — integrate with real sensor data from mill equipment, use CV for fruit quality grading, and optimize throughput. Most Indonesian mills still operate without digital process monitoring.
