# FinRiskAtlas — Decision-Aligned Evaluation of LLMs for Financial Risk Review
**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25325v1

## Summary
FinRiskAtlas evaluates LLMs for professional financial risk review — not just general financial competence, but the specific review operations required by regulated workflows. Published Aug 26, 2026. Provides a benchmark for deploying LLMs in compliance-critical financial contexts.

## Idea angle
Financial risk review is a regulated activity where errors have real consequences. FinRiskAtlas's decision-aligned evaluation — testing whether the LLM performs the exact review operation the workflow requires — is the right evaluation paradigm. General finance benchmarks (like CFA-style questions) don't capture workflow-specific accuracy.
