# AlphaSchema: LLM-Based Alpha Mining for Trading Factor Discovery
**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Keyword:** stock-trading
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26642v1

## Summary
AlphaSchema explores the space of trading semantics for LLM-based alpha mining — using language models to automatically discover and generate trading factors. It addresses the problem of existing LLM-based systems that delegate both factor construction and search decisions to the agent without explicit exploration spaces.

## Idea angle
Apply AlphaSchema's approach to Indonesian market factors — IDX has unique alpha sources (commodity cycles, political events, rupiah movements) that LLMs could mine. The gap: alpha factor research on IDX is manual and limited; an automated LLM-driven factor discovery system could surface novel signals specific to Indonesian market dynamics.

## Details
- Key concept: explicit exploration space for LLM-based factor generation
- Focus: principled alpha mining with structured search
- Addresses: unprincipled delegation of construction + search to agents
