# Sectoral Inter-Dependencies Drive Loss of Structural Balance in Signed Financial Networks
**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12023v1

## Summary
This paper models financial markets as signed graphs where cooperation and conflict coexist, showing how sectoral inter-dependencies cause structural balance loss. It applies concepts from psychological balance theory to financial networks, revealing how sector relationships shift over time.

## Idea angle
Build a signed network model of Indonesian stock market sectors — IDX has strong inter-sector dependencies (banking-commodities, banking-property, consumer-retail). The gap: understanding these network dynamics could improve sector rotation strategies and systemic risk monitoring for OJK (Indonesian financial regulator).

## Details
- Authors: Kartik Dahake, Abhijit Chakraborty
- Key concept: signed graphs, structural balance theory applied to financial networks
- Application: understanding cooperation/conflict dynamics in financial markets
