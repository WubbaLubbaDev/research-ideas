## Summary
Maybe is an open-source, self-hosted personal finance app that tracks net worth, spending, and investments. It aggregates bank accounts, investment portfolios, and budgeting into a single dashboard. After a failed VC-backed startup attempt, it was revived as a community-driven OSS project.

## Idea angle
Fork Maybe for the Indonesian market — integrate with local bank APIs (BCA, Mandiri), e-wallets (GoPay, OVO), and IDX brokerages. The gap: no self-hosted personal finance tool supports Indonesian financial institutions. Most local alternatives are SaaS-only with no data ownership. This would be valuable for privacy-conscious Indonesian users.
