## Summary
Mondelez (parent of Cadbury, Oreo) cut ties with 12 palm oil suppliers citing deforestation concerns. This move demonstrates how major FMCG companies are enforcing NDPE (No Deforestation, No Peat, No Exploitation) policies in their supply chains, creating supply chain traceability requirements.

## Idea angle
Build a supply chain traceability platform for Indonesian CPO that helps smallholders prove compliance with NDPE policies. The gap: Indonesian smallholders risk being cut from global supply chains if they can't demonstrate deforestation-free practices. A blockchain or GPS-based traceability system would help them maintain market access.
