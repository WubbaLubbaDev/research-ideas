# Trading Across Brokerages — Unified Trading Platform
**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Keyword:** stock trading
**Source:** Product Hunt
**URL:** https://www.producthunt.com/products/trading-across-brokerages/awards

## Summary
A platform that lets users see all assets in one place and trade across multiple brokerages without switching apps. Aggregates portfolio views and enables cross-brokerage trading from a single interface.

## Idea angle
Build this for Indonesian brokerages — most Indonesian retail investors use 2-3 brokers (IDX + crypto + US stocks). A unified dashboard connecting Sekuritas, crypto exchanges, and international brokers would solve real pain.
