# Sequential Inference for Win-Martingales in Prediction Markets
**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Keyword:** stock trading
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12291v1

## Summary
This paper studies when a decision-maker should stop observing a prediction market price process, modeling prices as 'win-martingales' — probability processes for binary events. It provides optimal stopping rules for sequential inference in prediction markets.

## Idea angle
Apply win-martingale stopping theory to IDX intraday trading — Indonesian market has distinct intraday patterns (morning rally, lunch lull, afternoon reversal) that could be modeled as sequential decision problems. Optimal stopping rules for when to enter/exit based on observed price evolution could improve systematic trading strategies.

## Details
Authors: Steven Campbell, Karl Kristian Engelund. arXiv: 2608.12291.
