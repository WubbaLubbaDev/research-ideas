## Summary
An iOS app that acts as an AI personal finance coach, guiding users through financial decisions with a non-judgmental, conversational interface. Rated 3.2/5 on Product Hunt.

## Idea angle
The 'non-judgmental finance coach' angle is interesting — most finance apps make users feel bad about spending. A coaching model that frames decisions as tradeoffs rather than failures could improve adherence. The low rating (3.2) suggests the execution gap, not the concept gap — there's room to do better.
