**Date:** 2026-08-16

**Source:** Hacker News

## Summary
Stanford's CS007 is a course teaching personal finance specifically for engineers — covering investing, retirement planning, equity compensation, and tax optimization. The course materials are publicly available as a blog, making elite financial education accessible beyond Stanford's campus.

## Idea angle
Build a similar localized personal finance curriculum for Indonesian tech workers. The gap: Indonesian engineers lack structured financial education tailored to their compensation structure (RSUs, startup equity, IDR-based salary, BPJS/THT). A free online course adapted for Indonesian tax law and investment products (mutual funds, SBN, IDX stocks) would fill a real void.
