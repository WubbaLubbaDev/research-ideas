# Paisa — Open-Source Personal Finance Manager
**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** Hacker News
**URL:** https://paisa.fyi/

## Summary
Paisa is an open-source personal finance manager that uses plain-text accounting principles, making it possible to track finances with full data ownership and no cloud dependency.

## Idea angle
Plain-text accounting is a power-user niche that could be modernized with AI — an LLM that categorizes transactions, predicts cash flow, and generates natural-language financial reports from a ledger file. The local-first approach also addresses privacy concerns.
