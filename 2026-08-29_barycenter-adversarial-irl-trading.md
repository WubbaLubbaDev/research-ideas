# Barycenter-Based Adversarial Inverse RL for Stock Trading Policies
**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Keyword:** stock trading
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15770v1

## Summary
A paper proposing barycenter-based adversarial inverse reinforcement learning for stock trading — learning trading policies from expert demonstrations rather than from reward design. Addresses delayed rewards, poor exploration, and risk constraint enforcement. Published Aug 16, 2026.

## Idea angle
Inverse RL — learning from expert behavior rather than designing rewards — is more robust for trading because good reward functions are notoriously hard to specify. The barycenter approach handles multi-modal expert strategies (different traders have different styles). Could be used to learn from successful IDX traders' historical behavior.
