# ProjectionLab: Personal Finance Simulator (2 years in the making)
**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** Hacker News
**URL:** https://projectionlab.com/

## Summary
ProjectionLab is a personal finance simulator that took 2 years to build, allowing users to model long-term financial scenarios with Monte Carlo simulations, retirement planning, and portfolio projections. Reached 766 points on HN.

## Idea angle
Build a retirement planner tailored to Indonesia's BPJS-TK pension system, which is fundamentally different from US 401k/Social Security. Most Indonesian workers don't understand their pension benefits — a visual simulator showing projected BPJS payouts plus private investment scenarios would fill a real gap.

HN: 766 points, 288 comments. Solo developer, 2-year build.
