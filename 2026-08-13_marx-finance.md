## Summary
Marx Finance deploys multiple AI agents that debate market analysis and investment theses in real-time, creating a multi-perspective view of financial markets through agent disagreement and consensus.

## Idea angle
Build a similar multi-agent debate platform for IDX analysis where agents represent different investment styles (value, growth, momentum, contrarian) and debate specific Indonesian stocks. The debate format could expose cognitive biases in single-model analysis — especially useful in a market dominated by herd behavior and retail momentum.
