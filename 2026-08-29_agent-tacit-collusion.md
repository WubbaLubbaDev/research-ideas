# AI Agents in Algorithmic Markets: On the Emergence of Tacit Collusion
**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Keyword:** AI agent
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26896v1

## Summary
A paper showing that as electricity market participants adopt learning-based agents for bidding, markets become algorithmic — and evidence from other domains shows these agents can develop tacit collusion without explicit coordination. Published Aug 27, 2026.

## Idea angle
Tacit collusion in algorithmic markets is an emerging regulatory concern. If trading agents learn to implicitly cooperate (e.g., all agents learn to avoid price wars), markets become less competitive. This applies directly to algorithmic stock trading — if most traders use similar RL agents, they may converge on collusive behavior without anyone intending it.
