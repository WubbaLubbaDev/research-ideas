# LLM-Driven Small-Cap Trading: Financial News Sentiment + Macro + Technical Signals
**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12283v1

## Summary
This paper studies an uncertainty-aware portfolio construction that feeds LLM-predicted risk (decomposed into aleatoric and epistemic components) into small-cap trading strategies, integrating financial news sentiment, macroeconomic indicators, and technical signals.

## Idea angle
Apply this uncertainty-aware LLM approach to IDX small-cap stocks, which are under-researched and often mispriced. Indonesian small-caps have less analyst coverage and thinner liquidity — an LLM that can extract signal from local news (Bisnis Indonesia, Kontan) while quantifying prediction uncertainty could find genuine alpha in neglected names.

## Details
Authors: Alireza Kargarzadeh, Nariman Khaledian, Navid Parvini. arXiv: 2608.12283.
