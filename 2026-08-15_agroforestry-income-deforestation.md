# When Does Agroforestry Income Reduce Deforestation? Evidence from Madagascar
**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Keyword:** palm oil
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13706v1

## Summary
A natural experiment using Madagascar's 2015 vanilla price boom to show that income gains reduced deforestation by 1.7 percentage points in 2017 — but only in drier, accessible areas. In wetter, high-agricultural-potential areas, income gains actually increased deforestation.

## Idea angle
The heterogeneous effect of income on deforestation is a critical insight for palm oil policy. Could build a predictive model that identifies which Indonesian palm oil districts are at highest risk of income-driven deforestation, enabling targeted sustainability interventions before clearing happens.

## Details
Authors: Camille DeSisto et al. (Rice/Yale) | Submitted: Mar 2026 | Uses matching-augmented synthetic control design. ~701 hectares of avoided forest loss in 2017.
