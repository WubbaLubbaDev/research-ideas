## Summary
An IoT-driven precision agriculture system for Indonesian palm oil estates that uses ML.FORECAST to predict FFB yield per block, Dynamic Tables for real-time harvest dashboards, and Cortex AI for generating agronomist recommendations.

## Idea angle
Block-level FFB yield prediction combined with agronomist recommendations could be productized for Indonesian smallholders — a mobile app that uses weather data, soil sensors, and historical yield data to recommend optimal harvest timing and fertilizer application.
