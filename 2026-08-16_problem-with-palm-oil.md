## Summary
An analysis of the sustainability problems with palm oil production — deforestation, biodiversity loss, carbon emissions from peatland conversion, and social issues. The article argues that while boycotting palm oil is counterproductive (it's the most land-efficient vegetable oil), the solution lies in sustainable production and certification.

## Idea angle
Build an RSPO certification readiness tool for Indonesian smallholders. The gap: RSPO certification is complex and costly for smallholders to achieve; a digital tool that guides them through certification steps, tracks compliance, and connects them with certified supply chains would help both smallholders and sustainability goals.
