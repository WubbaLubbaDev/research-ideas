## Summary
Doji is a stock market options analysis tool that helps traders find optimal buy and sell opportunities using options flow data and technical analysis.

## Idea angle
IDX doesn't have a liquid options market yet, but Indonesia's financial regulators are exploring derivatives expansion. Building options analysis infrastructure ahead of IDX options launch would position a product to capture early demand. Start with warrants and structured products already trading on IDX.
