## Summary
Doji is a platform that helps traders find the best options to buy and sell on the stock market. It provides tools for options analysis, screening, and strategy building — making options trading more accessible to retail investors.

## Idea angle
Build an options analysis tool for IDX — Indonesian options market is nascent but growing (IDX launched options in 2023). The gap: no Indonesian-language options analysis platform exists. First-mover advantage in educating Indonesian retail investors about options trading could be significant as the market develops.
