**Date:** 2026-08-13

## Summary
Robinhood's early $110M raise at $1.3B valuation marked the beginning of commission-free stock trading disrupting the brokerage industry. HN discussion covered the sustainability of zero-commission models.

## Idea angle
Indonesian brokers have already gone to near-zero commissions (Stockbit, Bibit, Ajaib), but the UX gap vs. Robinhood is still enormous. Building a Robinhood-quality mobile-first trading experience for IDX — with instant settlement, fractional shares, and social features — specifically targeting Gen Z Indonesian investors could capture the next wave of retail market participation.
