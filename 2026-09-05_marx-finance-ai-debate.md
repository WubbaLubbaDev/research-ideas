**Date:** 2026-09-05

## Summary
A Product Hunt finance product where multiple AI agents debate market analysis and investment theses. Each agent takes a different analytical stance (bull, bear, technical, fundamental) and presents arguments.

## Idea angle
Multi-agent debate for market analysis is a novel interaction model — instead of one AI giving you a prediction, you watch agents argue from different angles. Building an open-source version with configurable agent personas (value investor, momentum trader, macro analyst) could democratize what Marx Finance charges for.
