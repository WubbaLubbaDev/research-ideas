# Open Benchmark Dataset for GeoAI Foundation Models for Oil Palm Mapping in Indonesia
**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Keyword:** palm oil
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08303v1

## Summary
An open benchmark dataset for GeoAI foundation models focused on oil palm mapping in Indonesia. Addresses the fact that oil palm cultivation is a leading cause of deforestation in Indonesia, and detailed mapping is needed to support sustainability efforts. Published Sep 2025.

## Idea angle
Open benchmark datasets are how ML communities rally around a problem. This one enables any researcher to train and evaluate oil palm detection models on standardized Indonesian data. For SawitPRO or similar agritech platforms, this dataset could directly improve plantation boundary detection and deforestation monitoring.
