## Summary
A free, ad-free trading journal app focused on speed and ease of use. Lets traders log trades, track performance, and review patterns without subscription fees.

## Idea angle
Trading journals are the most underbuilt category in retail trading — most are either expensive SaaS or clunky spreadsheets. An open-source, self-hostable trading journal with automatic broker API import and AI-powered pattern detection (identifying recurring mistakes) would be a strong differentiator.
