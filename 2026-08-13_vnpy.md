## Summary
vnpy is a Python-based open-source quantitative trading platform development framework, widely used in China for algorithmic trading across futures, stocks, options, and crypto markets.

## Idea angle
Build an IDX broker connector for vnpy — Indonesia's algorithmic trading ecosystem is underdeveloped, and most local brokers (Mirae, Indo Premier, BNI Sekuritas) offer APIs but lack open-source integration. A vnpy connector for Indonesian brokers would enable local quants to backtest and deploy strategies without building infrastructure from scratch.
