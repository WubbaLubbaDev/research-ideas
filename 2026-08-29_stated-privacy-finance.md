# Stated — Privacy-First AI Finance Tracker
**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** Product Hunt
**URL:** https://www.producthunt.com/products/stated-2

## Summary
A privacy-first AI finance tracker on Product Hunt that works by uploading bank statement PDFs — no bank login, no personal info, no risk. AI extracts spending insights and budget recommendations from the statement data alone.

## Idea angle
Privacy-first finance is a strong differentiator in markets where bank API access is limited or consumers are wary of OAuth. The PDF-upload approach works everywhere — no Plaid integration needed. For Indonesia where open banking is nascent, this is the practical path to AI-powered financial insights.
