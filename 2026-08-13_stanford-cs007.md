## Summary
Stanford's CS007 course covers personal finance specifically for engineers — equity compensation, investing, tax optimization, and financial planning. The course material is freely available online and reached 1089 points on HN.

## Idea angle
Create a localized personal finance course/tool for Indonesian tech workers who lack access to such education. Focus on IDX-specific topics: local tax structures (PPh), employee stock options (still rare in Indonesia), and property investment — the default investment vehicle for Indonesian professionals.
