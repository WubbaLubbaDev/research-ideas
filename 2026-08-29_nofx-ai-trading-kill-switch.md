## Summary
A 12.7K-star AI trading assistant that emphasizes safety with a built-in kill switch — the ability to immediately halt all trading activity. Created to address the growing concern of autonomous AI agents making unchecked financial decisions.

## Idea angle
The kill switch pattern is essential for any autonomous trading system. As AI trading agents proliferate, the ability to hard-stop execution is non-negotiable. This repo's approach — building the kill switch as a first-class architectural feature, not an afterthought — is a template for safe agentic systems in any domain.
