# Understanding the Vegetable Oil Debate via Social Media Analysis
**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Keyword:** palm-oil
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2308.07108v1

## Summary
This study analyzes over 20 million tweets related to vegetable oils to understand public opinion dynamics around palm oil, coconut oil, and olive oil. It reveals that palm oil dominates social media discussions, driven by health concerns, environmental activism, and trade policy debates.

## Idea angle
Build a real-time social media sentiment monitor for palm oil-related discussions in Indonesian and English, tracking how public perception affects CPO prices and policy decisions. The gap: Indonesian CPO exporters lack tools to anticipate consumer sentiment shifts in key markets (EU, India, China) that could affect demand and pricing.

## Details
- Dataset: 20+ million tweets
- Key finding: palm oil dominates vegetable oil discussions
- Topics: health concerns, environmental activism, trade policy
- Method: large-scale social media sentiment analysis
