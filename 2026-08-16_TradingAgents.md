## Summary
TradingAgents is a multi-agent LLM framework for financial trading that uses specialized AI agents (analyst, researcher, trader, risk manager) to simulate a real trading firm's decision-making process. Each agent has a distinct role — from fundamental analysis to risk assessment — and they collaborate to produce trading decisions.

## Idea angle
Build a lightweight version of this multi-agent framework for retail traders in Indonesia, focused on IDX stocks. The gap: most LLM trading frameworks target US markets; an IDX-specific version with local market data feeds (BIHTG, IDX-RSS) and Indonesian-language news sentiment would be genuinely novel. Could also integrate with existing broker APIs (Stockbit, Bibit).
