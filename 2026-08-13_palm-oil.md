## arXiv
### [Oil price shocks reveal unequal capacities for mobility adaptation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12281v1)
**Authors:** Zihao Zhang, Yuanbo Zhang, Xiaolei Ma et al. | **Published:** 2026-08-12
**Abstract summary:** Urban decarbonization raises travel costs, and oil price shocks reveal which neighborhoods can adapt. The study leverages fuel price variations to understand mobility resilience across different urban demographics.
**Idea angle:** Palm oil-based biofuel mandates affect fuel prices in Indonesia — build a model showing how CPO price shocks impact transportation costs across Indonesian regions and income groups.

### [A Methodological Approach For Design Of Energy Efficient Urban Logistics Systems](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11853v1)
**Authors:** Emilienne Lardy, Mariam Lafkihi, Eric Ballot | **Published:** 2026-08-12
**Abstract summary:** Energy consumption in logistics is a major challenge, and France targets net-zero by 2050. The paper presents a methodological framework for designing energy-efficient urban logistics systems.
**Idea angle:** Palm oil logistics (from plantation to mill to port) is energy-intensive — apply the energy-efficient logistics framework to optimize CPO supply chain routing and reduce emissions.

### [T1, T2, and complex permittivities of hydrogels, paramagnetic salt solutions, and oils](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11117v1)
**Authors:** H Michael Gach, Kerri Prinos, Hannah Sechaga | **Published:** 2026-08-11
**Abstract summary:** MRI phantom constituents (hydrogels, paramagnetic electrolytes, and oils) were characterized for T1, T2, and complex permittivities. Oils including vegetable oils were measured for medical imaging calibration.
**Idea angle:** Palm oil's unique MRI properties could be used for quality grading — a non-destructive palm oil quality assessment tool using dielectric or magnetic properties to detect adulteration.
