# Future of Work with AI Agents: Auditing Automation and Augmentation Potential
**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Keyword:** AI agent
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06576

## Summary
A paper introducing a novel auditing framework to assess which occupational tasks workers want AI agents to automate or augment. Introduces the Human Agency Scale (HAS) and constructs the WORKBank database from 1,500 domain workers across 104 occupations.

## Idea angle
The Human Agency Scale is a practical tool for designing agent systems — it quantifies how much human involvement users want for each task. For a trading platform, this means some users want full automation while others want AI-assisted decision-making with a human pull-the-trigger step.

## Details
Authors: Yijia Shao, Erik Brynjolfsson, Diyi Yang et al. (Stanford) | 1,500 domain workers, 844 tasks, 104 occupations | Four zones: Green Light, Red Light, R&D Opportunity, Low Priority.
