**Date:** 2026-08-15

**Source:** Product Hunt

## Summary
AgentOS is a platform for running multiple AI agents across real projects with task management, workspaces, and organizational structure — designed for when running a single agent isn't enough.

## Idea angle
Multi-agent orchestration is the natural evolution for finance teams — one agent handles market research, another manages portfolio rebalancing, a third handles compliance reporting. AgentOS-style workspace isolation prevents cross-contamination of agent contexts.

## Details
Product Hunt launch. Positions agents as 'running like a company' with role-based task delegation.
