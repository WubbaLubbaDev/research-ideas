## Summary
StockSharp is an open-source algorithmic trading platform supporting stock markets, forex, crypto, and options. It provides a full trading robot development pipeline — from strategy design to execution — with connectors to major exchanges and brokerages.

## Idea angle
Build a StockSharp connector for Indonesian brokers (Stockbit, MIFX, Phintraco). The gap: Indonesian algorithmic traders lack a mature, open-source platform with local broker connectivity. StockSharp's C# architecture and extensive connector framework make it well-suited for adapting to IDX FIX protocol used by Indonesian exchanges.
