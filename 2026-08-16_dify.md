## Summary
Dify is an open-source platform for building agentic workflows, RAG pipelines, and AI applications with rich model and tool support. It provides a collaborative workspace for teams to prototype and deploy AI agents on cloud, VPC, or self-hosted infrastructure without rebuilding the stack.

## Idea angle
Deploy Dify as a self-hosted AI agent platform for Indonesian enterprises concerned about data sovereignty. The gap: Indonesian companies in regulated industries (banking, government) cannot use cloud-hosted AI platforms due to data residency requirements. A self-hosted Dify deployment with Indonesian language support and local LLM hosting would solve this.
