# Post-Training Quantization for Financial Time-Series Forecasting
**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12259v1

## Summary
This paper addresses post-training quantization (PTQ) for financial time-series forecasting models, enabling low-precision inference without retraining. It tackles the challenge of reliable activation quantization in financial models where distribution shifts are common.

## Idea angle
Quantized financial models could enable real-time trading signal generation on edge devices or low-cost cloud instances — relevant for Indonesian retail traders who can't afford expensive GPU infrastructure. A quantized model running on a $5/month VPS that generates daily IDX signals would be genuinely useful.
