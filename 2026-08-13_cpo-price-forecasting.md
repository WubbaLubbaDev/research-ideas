## Summary
A machine learning project for multivariate time series forecasting of Crude Palm Oil (CPO) prices, using multiple input variables to predict future CPO price movements.

## Idea angle
Build a production CPO price forecasting service for Indonesian palm oil producers and traders. CPO price volatility directly impacts 16+ million Indonesian livelihoods. A forecasting tool that incorporates weather data, Malaysian futures, Indonesian export policies, and biodiesel mandate changes could help producers optimize selling timing.
