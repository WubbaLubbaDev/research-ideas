# Agentic Approach for Travel Behavior Modeling and Weather-Sensitive Demand Prediction
**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Keyword:** AI agent
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20320v1

## Summary
A three-agent workflow integrating conversational data collection, structured data processing, and behavioral prediction. Uses a chatbot-administered survey with image augmentation to collect mode choices across weather scenarios.

## Idea angle
The three-agent pattern (collect, process, predict) is directly applicable to market research — build agents that survey Indonesian consumers, process responses, and predict demand patterns. The weather-sensitivity angle maps to commodity (palm oil) demand.

## Details
Authors: Narges Ahmadi, Yubo Jiao, Jônatas Augusto Manzolli. Published Aug 20, 2026. 454 respondent-scenario observations.
