## Summary
Hey Noah is the #1 product of August 2026 on Product Hunt — a proactive AI executive assistant designed for founders. Handles scheduling, email triage, research, and follow-ups autonomously, not just responding to requests.

## Idea angle
Proactive AI assistants that anticipate needs (vs. reactive chatbots) are the next UX paradigm. For founders, an agent that pre-drafts responses, pre-screens meetings, and proactively flags issues is worth paying for. The 'proactive' design pattern — agent initiates rather than waits — applies to trading agents too (alerting before you ask).
