## Summary
SignalForge combines Markov chains, Kalman filters, and AI agents for stock screening, portfolio construction, and SEC filing analysis. It targets hedge fund-style market intelligence with LLM-powered analysis of financial documents.

## Idea angle
Build an SEC-filing-style analyzer for IDX disclosures (annual reports, material announcements) using LLMs to extract signals Indonesian investors miss. The Indonesian market has less analyst coverage — an AI-powered filing reader could surface alpha from under-analyzed corporate disclosures.
