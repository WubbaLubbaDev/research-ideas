# OpenBB: Open Data Platform for analysts, quants and AI agents
**Date:** 2026-08-13

## Summary
OpenBB is an open-source financial data platform that aggregates market data from multiple sources and exposes it through a unified API, CLI, and SDK. It's designed for quants, analysts, and AI agents who need programmatic access to financial data.

## Idea angle
Use OpenBB as the data backbone for a personal investment research dashboard targeting Indonesian retail investors. Most IDX data tools are paid/walled — an OpenBB-based aggregator that pulls from free sources (Yahoo Finance, BI feeds) could democratize access for the local market.
