## Summary
A Show HN project where the author built an open-source AI quant agent that trades live with their own $1000. Demonstrates end-to-end autonomous trading with real money on the line. Showcased the practical challenges of deploying LLM-driven trading in production.

## Idea angle
Skin-in-the-game trading projects are the most honest benchmarks. Backtests lie; live trading with real money reveals the gap between theory and execution. Studying this repo's failure modes (slippage, API rate limits, emotional override) is more valuable than any backtested framework.
