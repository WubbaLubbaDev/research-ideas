# AVA-Encoder: Agent-Native Video Representation Learning
**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Keyword:** AI agent
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12313v1

## Summary
AVA-Encoder introduces a structured video representation for agentic reasoning — enabling AI agents to learn from and reason about cinematic-grade video content, bridging the gap between video understanding and agentic manipulation.

## Idea angle
Apply agent-native video understanding to surveillance and monitoring — agents that can reason about video feeds (CCTV, drone footage) for security, traffic, or agricultural monitoring. In palm oil plantations, drone video analysis agents could detect harvest readiness and tree health autonomously.

## Details
Authors: Chuyue Li, Jinpeng Yu, Haozhe Wang. arXiv: 2608.12313.
