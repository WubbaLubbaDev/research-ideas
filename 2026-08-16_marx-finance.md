# Marx Finance: AI Agents Debate the Markets
**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** Product Hunt
**URL:** https://www.producthunt.com/products/marx-finance

## Summary
Marx Finance is a platform where multiple AI agents debate market conditions and investment decisions. Rather than relying on a single AI, it creates adversarial dialogue between agents with different analytical perspectives — fundamental, technical, macro — to surface blind spots and produce more robust conclusions.

## Idea angle
Build an adversarial AI debate system for Indonesian market analysis where agents argue over IDX positions — one bullish, one bearish, one macro-focused. The gap: retail Indonesian investors lack access to multi-perspective analysis. A debate format also makes financial education more engaging.
