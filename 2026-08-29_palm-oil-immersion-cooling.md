# Malaysia Launches Palm Oil-Based Immersion Cooling for Data Centers
**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Keyword:** palm oil
**Source:** Hacker News
**URL:** https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/malaysia-to-launch-palm-oil-based-immersion-cooling-fluid-for-data-centers/

## Summary
Malaysia is launching palm oil-based immersion cooling fluid for data centers — replacing synthetic dielectric fluids with a renewable, locally-produced alternative. Posted Aug 15, 2026. Taps into Malaysia's position as the world's second-largest palm oil producer.

## Idea angle
Finding non-food industrial applications for palm oil diversifies revenue streams and reduces dependence on volatile edible oil markets. Data center cooling is a booming market (AI infrastructure demand). If the thermal performance matches synthetic fluids, this creates a premium-grade use case for palm oil that could justify higher prices for certified sustainable supply.
