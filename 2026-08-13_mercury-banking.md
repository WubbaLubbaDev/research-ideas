## Summary
Mercury provides startup banking with APIs, virtual cards, treasury management, and FDIC-insured accounts. It's rated as one of the top finance products on Product Hunt with 4.8 rating.

## Idea angle
Indonesia lacks a developer-friendly startup banking API. Most local banks offer clunky interfaces and no API access for programmatic financial management. A Mercury-equivalent for Indonesian startups — with API-first account management, virtual cards for team spend, and auto-reconciliation — would be a massive enabler for the local startup ecosystem.
