# Calibration-Induced Degeneracy in LLM Financial Forecasting
**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20304

## Summary
This paper audits LLM-based financial forecasting and finds that calibration-induced degeneracy causes models to produce overconfident predictions that fail under market stress. An audit-trailed case study on next-day market risk.

## Idea angle
Build a calibration audit toolkit for any LLM-based financial prediction system — detect when models are overconfident before deployment. The gap: most LLM trading benchmarks measure returns, not calibration quality.

Author: Arin Mohanty. 13 pages, 2 figures, 6 tables. Published Aug 2026. Subjects: Risk Management.
