## Summary
A Show HN personal finance app that focuses on forward-looking projections rather than just tracking past spending. Posted Aug 29, 2026. Aimed at users who want to model future financial scenarios, not just see what already happened.

## Idea angle
Most personal finance apps are backward-looking (tracking what you spent). Forward-looking projection — 'if I save this much, here's my trajectory' — is what users actually need. Building this with LLM-powered scenario analysis ('what if I switch jobs?') would be the next evolution.
