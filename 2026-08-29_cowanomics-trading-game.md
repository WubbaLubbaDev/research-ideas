## Summary
A stock trading party game that uses web sockets for real-time multiplayer trading simulation. Posted Aug 27, 2026. Turns trading into a social game — players compete in real-time with fake money on real market dynamics.

## Idea angle
Gamified trading education is an underserved angle. Most trading education is dry (courses, textbooks) or too real (paper trading on real platforms). A party-game format could onboard new traders — especially in emerging markets where financial literacy is low. The websocket-based real-time architecture is reusable.
