# Regime-Gated Residual Mixture-of-Experts for Cross-Sectional Volatility Forecasting
**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12251v1

## Summary
This paper studies where regime information should enter a neural cross-sectional volatility forecasting model, using a mixture-of-experts architecture gated by market regimes. It forecasts 5-day realized volatility for 1,027 US stocks.

## Idea angle
Adapt regime-gated volatility forecasting to IDX, where market regimes are more volatile (political events, commodity cycles, capital flows). Indonesia's market shifts between 'risk-on commodities rally' and 'risk-off capital flight' regimes more sharply than developed markets — a model that explicitly detects and adapts to these regimes could improve risk management significantly.

Authors: Junyi Ye, Gargi Vijay Borde. arXiv: 2608.12251.
