# Entropic Factor Model for Robust Portfolio Replication
**Date:** 2026-09-05
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03552

## Summary
A two-stage methodology using information theory for robust portfolio replication. Uses Fermi-Dirac entropy to estimate parameters within empirical bounds and determine optimal weight replication. Outperforms OLS during COVID-19 crash by acting as a probabilistic 'circuit breaker' against compromised assets.

## Idea angle
The 'circuit breaker' behavior during stress events is the key insight — a portfolio construction method that automatically de-allocates from corrupted assets could be valuable for risk management tools. Building this as a drop-in replacement for OLS-based portfolio optimization in existing quant libraries would be immediately useful.
