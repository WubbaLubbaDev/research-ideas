# A Community Palm Model: Land Cover Dynamics for Supply Chains
**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Keyword:** palm-oil
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2405.09530v2

## Summary
A community palm oil model that provides timely information on land cover dynamics in supply shed areas. It addresses the difficulty of obtaining reliable data on deforestation, plantation expansion, and land use change — enabling stakeholders to track sustainability commitments and supply chain compliance.

## Idea angle
Integrate this community palm model with Indonesian satellite imagery (LAPAN, Planet Labs) to build a deforestation monitoring dashboard for RSPO compliance. The gap: Indonesian smallholders need affordable ways to demonstrate their land hasn't been newly deforested. Satellite-based monitoring with automated alerts would help both compliance and enforcement.

## Details
- Focus: land cover dynamics, supply chain transparency
- Problem: difficulty obtaining reliable data on plantation expansion
- Application: sustainability tracking, supply chain compliance
