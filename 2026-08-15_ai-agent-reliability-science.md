# Towards a Science of AI Agent Reliability
**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Keyword:** AI agent
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16666

## Summary
A paper proposing twelve concrete metrics that decompose agent reliability along four dimensions: consistency, robustness, predictability, and safety. Evaluating 15 models across two benchmarks, it finds that recent capability gains have only yielded small improvements in reliability.

## Idea angle
The reliability gap between benchmark performance and real-world deployment is the core problem for production agents. Could build a reliability testing harness for financial agents that runs strategy execution 100 times and measures variance in outcomes, failure modes, and edge-case behavior — before going live.

## Details
Authors: Stephan Rabanser, Sayash Kapoor, Arvind Narayanan et al. | Accepted at ICML 2026 | Interactive dashboard at hal.cs.princeton.edu/reliability
