# From Noisy Historical Maps to Time-Series Oil Palm Mapping
**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Keyword:** palm oil
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/2604.23776v1

## Summary
A method for generating time-series oil palm plantation maps from noisy historical data without manual annotation. Covers Malaysia and Indonesia (2020-2024) using satellite imagery and self-supervised learning.

## Idea angle
Extend the time-series mapping to include productivity estimates — combine plantation maps with yield data to forecast regional CPO production. This would give traders and policymakers a leading indicator of supply.

## Details
Covers Malaysia and Indonesia, 2020-2024. Uses self-supervised learning to avoid annotation costs. Satellite imagery based.
