## Summary
DeerFlow is ByteDance's open-source deep research multi-agent framework that orchestrates LLM-powered agents for complex research tasks, with podcast generation and multi-agent coordination capabilities.

## Idea angle
Adapt DeerFlow's multi-agent research architecture for market intelligence — agents that independently research different aspects of a company (financials, news, social sentiment, supply chain) and synthesize findings. This could be a powerful tool for equity research in markets with limited analyst coverage like IDX.
