## Summary
Sawit.jl is a semi-mechanistic oil palm growth and yield model written in Julia, a significant update to its predecessor PySawit. It models the biological growth processes of oil palm trees to predict yield.

## Idea angle
Integrate Sawit.jl's growth model with climate and soil data APIs to create a yield prediction tool for Indonesian plantations. Most smallholders have no yield forecasting — a tool that predicts expected yield based on plantation age, soil type, rainfall, and management practices could help with financial planning and loan applications.
