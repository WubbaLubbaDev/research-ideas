# Open Benchmark Dataset for GeoAI — Oil Palm Plantations in Indonesia
**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Keyword:** palm oil
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08303

## Summary
An open-access geospatial dataset of oil palm plantations and related land cover types in Indonesia from 2020-2024. Designed as a benchmark for GeoAI foundation models to test plantation detection and land cover classification.

## Idea angle
Build a production plantation monitoring system on top of this benchmark dataset — train models to detect illegal clearing, monitor replanting cycles, and estimate plantation age. The open dataset removes the data acquisition barrier.

## Details
Authors: MW Wafiq et al. Cited by 3. Published 2025, still relevant in Aug 2026. Open-access, Indonesia-focused.
