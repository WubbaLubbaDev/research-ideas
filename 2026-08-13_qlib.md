## Summary
Qlib is Microsoft's open-source AI quant platform supporting supervised learning, market dynamics modeling, and reinforcement learning for quantitative investment research. It includes an RD-Agent that automates the R&D process for quant strategies.

## Idea angle
Leverage Qlib's RD-Agent to auto-discover alpha factors specific to IDX stocks. Most quant research targets US markets — adapting Qlib's pipeline to Indonesian market microstructure (thin liquidity, concentrated ownership) would expose unique alpha sources that US-centric models miss.
