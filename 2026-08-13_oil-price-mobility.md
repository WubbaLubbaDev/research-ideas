# Oil price shocks reveal unequal capacities for mobility adaptation
**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Keyword:** palm oil
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12281v1

## Summary
This paper leverages the 2026 US-Iran oil shock as a natural experiment to study how different neighborhoods adapt to oil price increases, using 1.7 trillion point-of-interest records to measure mobility changes.

## Idea angle
Apply similar natural experiment methodology to palm oil price shocks — when CPO prices spike, how does it affect Indonesian rural economies dependent on palm oil? Price shocks propagate through the supply chain from futures markets to smallholder incomes. A data-driven study of this propagation could inform policy on price stabilization and smallholder protection.

## Details
Authors: Zihao Zhang, Yuanbo Zhang, Xiaolei Ma. arXiv: 2608.12281. 1.7 trillion POI records.
