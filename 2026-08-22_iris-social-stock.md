## Summary
Iris is a social stock app that lets you see friends and family stock trades in real-time. Integrates with Robinhood to create a social investing experience where you can follow and discuss trades.

## Idea angle
Build a social trading app for Indonesian retail investors — the community aspect of Indonesian stock market forums (Stockbit, Telegram groups) is huge but fragmented. A structured social trading layer with verified trade sharing could capture this market.
