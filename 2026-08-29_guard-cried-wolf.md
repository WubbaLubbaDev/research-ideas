# The Guard That Cried Wolf: Over-Safety in Agent Guardrails
**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Keyword:** AI agent
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27009v1

## Summary
A paper on the over-safety problem in agent guardrails — guardrails that refuse legitimate actions because of scary keywords or patterns. Shows that over-safety blocks deployment of agent systems in production. Published Aug 27, 2026.

## Idea angle
Over-safety is the silent killer of agent adoption. A guardrail that refuses 30% of legitimate trades because they 'look risky' is worse than no guardrail — it trains users to bypass safety. The paper's contribution is quantifying this trade-off and providing a framework for tuning guardrail sensitivity.
