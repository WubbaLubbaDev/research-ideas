## Summary
Open-source tropical cover crop seed-rate, nitrogen-fixation, and climate-suitability calculators for oil palm, rubber, and smallholder mixed systems. Built by Chemiseed Sdn. Bhd. (Malaysia). Provides data-driven tools for sustainable agronomy in tropical agriculture.

## Idea angle
Cover crop optimization is an underserved but high-impact area — better ground cover means less erosion, better soil health, and reduced chemical input. These calculators could be integrated into a plantation management platform (like SawitPRO) as a decision-support module.
