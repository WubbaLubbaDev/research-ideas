# Game-Theoretic Modeling of Heterogeneous Investor Interactions for Stock Price Forecasting
**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Keyword:** stock-trading
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23953v1

## Summary
This paper models stock price forecasting using game theory to capture complex interactions among heterogeneous investors. Rather than treating stocks independently, it models the strategic interactions between different investor types, providing more reliable forecasting for quantitative trading.

## Idea angle
Apply game-theoretic investor interaction models to IDX, where retail dominance (70%+ of volume) creates unique strategic dynamics vs. institutional players. The gap: Indonesian market microstructure is fundamentally different from US/EU markets; game-theoretic models that capture retail-vs-institutional interactions would be especially relevant.

## Details
- Key concept: heterogeneous investor interaction modeling
- Approach: game theory applied to stock price prediction
- Focus: complex relationships among stocks and investors
