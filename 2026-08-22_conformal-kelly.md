# Conformal Kelly — Conformal Prediction for Position Sizing
**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01494

## Summary
Combines conformal prediction intervals with fractional Kelly criterion for position sizing. Uses statistical guarantees from conformal prediction to set the scale of Kelly bets, bridging rigorous uncertainty quantification with practical portfolio sizing.

## Idea angle
Implement this as a position-sizing module for retail trading bots — most retail tools use fixed position sizes or naive Kelly without uncertainty quantification. Conformal Kelly gives statistically grounded bet sizes.
