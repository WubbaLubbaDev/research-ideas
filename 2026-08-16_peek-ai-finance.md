**Date:** 2026-08-16

## Summary
Peek is an AI personal finance coach that guides users through financial decisions without judgment. It focuses on understanding spending patterns and building better habits through proactive, conversational guidance rather than dashboards and charts.

## Idea angle
Build an Indonesian-language AI finance coach integrated with local e-wallets (GoPay, OVO, DANA) and banking APIs. The gap: Indonesian personal finance apps are mostly manual entry; an AI coach that reads transaction data and proactively suggests actions in Bahasa Indonesia would be a differentiator.
