## Idea angle
Create a consumer-facing palm oil sustainability scoring app — scan a product's barcode and see its palm oil sourcing score, deforestation footprint, and RSPO certification status. Indonesian consumers are largely unaware of palm oil sustainability issues despite living in the world's largest producer country. A transparency tool could drive consumer awareness and producer accountability simultaneously.
