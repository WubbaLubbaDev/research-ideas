## Summary
A decentralized invoice financing protocol on the Stellar blockchain where SMEs tokenize unpaid invoices and lenders compete to discount them for instant liquidity.

## Idea angle
On-chain invoice factoring solves a real SME cash flow problem. Could build a dashboard that aggregates real-time factoring rates across multiple protocols and recommends the best discount terms based on invoice aging and debtor creditworthiness.
