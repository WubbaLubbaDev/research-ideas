## Summary
A New York Times Magazine deep-dive into how palm oil, initially promoted as a sustainable alternative to fossil fuels and a driver of rural development, became a major driver of deforestation and climate change in Borneo. The article traces the paradox of a commodity that is both economically essential and ecologically devastating.

## Idea angle
Build an educational interactive tool for Indonesian policymakers and consumers showing the trade-offs between CPO economic benefits and environmental costs. The gap: the palm oil debate is highly polarized; a data-driven, balanced visualization tool that quantifies both sides (GDP contribution vs. deforestation, smallholder income vs. CO2 emissions) would inform better policy.
