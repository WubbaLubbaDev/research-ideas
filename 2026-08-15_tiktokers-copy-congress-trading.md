# TikTokers Are Trading Stocks by Copying What Members of Congress Do
**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Keyword:** stock trading
**Source:** Hacker News

## Summary
An NPR article about TikTokers who track and copy the stock trades of members of Congress, using publicly available disclosure filings to mirror what lawmakers are buying and selling.

## Idea angle
The politician trading transparency movement reveals a gap in retail investor tooling — a real-time notification app that tracks lawmaker filings and lets users auto-copy trades. The social/viral dimension also suggests a community feature where users discuss and vote on which politicians to follow.
