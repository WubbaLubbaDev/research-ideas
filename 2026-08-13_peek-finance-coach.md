# Peek: AI personal finance coach
**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** Product Hunt
**URL:** https://www.producthunt.com/products/peek-15

## Summary
Peek is an AI-powered personal finance coach that guides users through budgeting, saving, and financial planning with a non-judgmental conversational interface designed for people who feel overwhelmed by finance apps.

## Idea angle
Build an Indonesian-language personal finance coach that understands local context — BPJS, e-wallets (GoPay, OVO, DANA), BNPL (Akulaku, Kredivo), and the informal economy. Existing finance apps are US/EU-centric; an AI coach that natively understands Indonesian financial products and cultural attitudes toward money (saving vs. face-saving consumption) would be genuinely novel.
