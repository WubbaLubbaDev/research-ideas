## Summary
A repository for large-scale oil palm mapping across Southeast Asia using Google Earth Engine, developing remote sensing approaches to detect and monitor oil palm plantations at regional scale.

## Idea angle
Build a real-time deforestation monitoring tool for Indonesian palm oil supply chains using GEE + satellite imagery. Companies like SawitPRO that need RSPO compliance verification could use automated plantation boundary detection to monitor supplier plots for encroachment into forest areas — a critical ESG requirement.
