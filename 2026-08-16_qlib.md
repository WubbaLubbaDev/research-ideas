## Summary
Qlib is Microsoft's open-source quant investment platform that uses AI/ML for quantitative research — from idea exploration to production. It supports supervised learning, market dynamics modeling, reinforcement learning, and integrates with RD-Agent for automated R&D pipelines.

## Idea angle
Adapt Qlib's RL-based portfolio optimization for IDX stocks. The platform's modular design supports custom data loaders — add IDX historical data and test RL agents on Jakarta Composite Index. The gap: most quant platforms are US/EU-focused; applying Qlib to emerging markets like Indonesia would reveal whether RL-based strategies transfer or need local calibration.
