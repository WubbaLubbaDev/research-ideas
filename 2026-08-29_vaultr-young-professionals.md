**Date:** 2026-08-29

## Summary
A personal finance app on Product Hunt built specifically for young professionals who 'earn well but feel financially lost.' Tracks transactions, sets budgets, and manages recurring bills. Targets the high-income-low-literacy demographic.

## Idea angle
The 'earns well but financially lost' segment is large and underserved — especially in tech hubs like Jakarta where young engineers earn above-average salaries but lack financial education. A localized version with IDX investment options and Indonesian tax planning would fill a real gap.
