# AI Financial Advice: Supply, Demand, and Life Cycle Implications
**Date:** 2026-08-22
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01607

## Summary
A comprehensive study on the supply and demand of AI-powered financial advice and its life cycle implications. Examines how AI advice changes saving, investing, and retirement planning behavior across demographics.

## Idea angle
Build an AI financial advisor for the Indonesian middle class — a demographic largely underserved by traditional financial advisors. Focus on sharia-compliant investment products and local tax-advantaged accounts.

## Details
Authors: Taha Choukhmane, Tim de Silva, Weidong Lin, Matthew Akuzawa. 92 pages, 38 figures, 10 tables. Published Aug 2026.
