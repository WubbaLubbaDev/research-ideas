# Comprehensive Database of Leaf Temperature, Water, and CO2 Fluxes in Young Oil Palm Plants
**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Keyword:** palm oil
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03308v1

## Summary
A comprehensive database of leaf temperature, water, and CO2 fluxes in young oil palm plants across diverse climate scenarios. Supports functional-structural plant models (FSPM) that replicate plant responses to their environment for climate change prediction. Published Jan 6, 2026.

## Idea angle
Physiological data on oil palm is scarce — this database fills a critical gap for building accurate growth and yield models under climate change. For precision agriculture, leaf-level flux data enables micro-climate-aware irrigation and fertilization recommendations. Could feed into an AI-driven plantation management system.
