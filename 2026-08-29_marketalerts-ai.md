## Summary
A Product Hunt stock trading tool that uses AI to monitor stocks and deliver real-time alerts. Analyzes any stock, uncovers rare market patterns as they emerge, and generates trade ideas. Positioned as an AI-first stock monitoring platform.

## Idea angle
Real-time AI pattern detection is table stakes for modern trading tools. The differentiator is reducing false positives — most alert systems drown users in noise. An AI that learns your trading style and only surfaces patterns relevant to your strategy would be genuinely useful.
