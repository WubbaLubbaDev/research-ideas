## Summary
A personal finance app on Product Hunt that works fully offline — no internet required. Tracks budget with category breakdowns, monitors stock & crypto portfolios, all stored locally. Prioritizes privacy and zero-dependency operation.

## Idea angle
Offline-first finance apps serve the privacy-conscious and those in unreliable-connectivity markets. For Indonesia, where internet reliability varies and privacy concerns are growing, an offline-first budgeting app with IDX stock tracking could find a niche. The local-storage approach also means no server costs — sustainable as a side project.
