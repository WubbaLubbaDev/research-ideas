# Teaching LLM Tutors to Withhold Answers: Socratic Supervisor Architecture
**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Keyword:** AI agent
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12292v1

## Summary
This paper presents a supervisor architecture and evidence-driven method for tuning LLM tutors to exhibit Socratic behavior — asking guiding questions instead of giving direct answers. Students using guarded Socratic tutors scored lower during practice but higher on post-tests.

## Idea angle
Build a Socratic AI tutor for Indonesian K-12 education (Kurikulum Merdeka) that helps students discover answers rather than giving them directly. Indonesian education culture is heavily rote-memorization based — a Socratic AI tutor that promotes critical thinking could be genuinely transformative for learning outcomes.

## Details
Authors: Yusuf Pisan. arXiv: 2608.12292. Randomized study showed Socratic guard improved post-test scores.
