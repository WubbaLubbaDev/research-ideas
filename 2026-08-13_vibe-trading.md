**Date:** 2026-08-13

## Summary
Vibe-Trading is an LLM-powered personal trading agent with 68+ tools, supporting backtesting across 9 market engines (US, HK, China, Korea, Japan, Canada, crypto), MCP integration, and live-trading safety guards. It includes alpha factor libraries (456 factors), shadow accounts, and research autopilot.

## Idea angle
Extend Vibe-Trading with an IDX market engine — Indonesian equities have unique microstructure (auto-rejection limits, circuit breakers, foreign ownership caps) that no existing tool models. Building an IDX backtest engine with proper handling of these constraints would be a genuine contribution to the open-source quant community.
