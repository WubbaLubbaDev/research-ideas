# FinRL-X: AI-Native Modular Infrastructure for Quantitative Trading
**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Keyword:** finance
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/2603.21330v1

## Summary
A modular trading architecture that unifies data processing, strategy construction, backtesting, and broker execution under a weight-centric interface, addressing the gap between research evaluation and live deployment.

## Idea angle
The deployment-consistency problem is real — most quant research fails in production because backtest and live execution semantics differ. FinRL-X's composable pipeline could be extended with an MCP layer that lets AI agents construct and deploy strategies without code changes between research and production.

## Details
Authors: Hongyang Yang et al. (AI4Finance Foundation) | Submitted: Mar 2026 | Supports both rule-based and AI-driven components including RL allocators and LLM sentiment signals.
