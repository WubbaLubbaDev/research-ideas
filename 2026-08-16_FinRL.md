## Summary
FinRL is a financial reinforcement learning framework that provides a standardized pipeline for building RL-based trading agents. It supports multiple markets (stock, crypto, forex) with built-in environment design, benchmark datasets, and modular agent architecture for algorithmic trading research.

## Idea angle
Adapt FinRL for IDX trading by creating a custom gym environment with Jakarta Composite Index data. The gap: RL-based trading research on emerging markets is scarce; testing whether RL agents trained on IDX data can discover alpha would be a valuable research contribution. Could also compare RL vs. traditional algorithmic strategies on Indonesian stocks.
