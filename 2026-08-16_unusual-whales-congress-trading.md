## Summary
Unusual Whales tracks and surfaces unusual stock trading activity by US Congress members, revealing potential conflicts of interest and insider trading. The platform provides real-time alerts on congressional trades, showing that lawmakers consistently outperform the market.

## Idea angle
Build a similar tracker for Indonesian government officials and DPR members who may have access to market-moving policy information. The gap: Indonesia lacks transparency on politician stock trading. While regulatory frameworks differ, tracking publicly disclosed transactions by officials with policy influence would serve both journalism and retail investor interests.
