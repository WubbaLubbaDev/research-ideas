## Summary
An MCP server delivering 41 tools for market data, SEC filings, macro and risk tooling across Webull, Saxo, and IBKR — with order execution behind a human approval step the assistant cannot reach.

## Idea angle
The human-in-the-loop execution gate is the key design pattern. Could extend this to a multi-broker MCP that adds risk limits, position sizing rules, and automated portfolio rebalancing with configurable approval thresholds.
