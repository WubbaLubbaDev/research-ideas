# The Attack and Defense Landscape of Agentic AI: A Comprehensive Survey
**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Keyword:** AI agent
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11088

## Summary
The first systematic and comprehensive survey of AI agent security, covering the design space, attack landscape, and defense mechanisms for secure AI agent systems. Includes case studies pointing out existing gaps and introduces a framework for understanding security risks.

## Idea angle
Agent security is an entire field that barely existed a year ago. The survey identifies prompt injection in tool-execution logic as a primary attack vector. Could build an agent firewall that sanitizes all tool inputs/outputs, blocks known injection patterns, and logs suspicious tool calls for audit.

## Details
Authors: Juhee Kim, Xiaoyuan Liu, Dawn Song et al. | Accepted at USENIX Security 2026 | First systematic survey of AI agent security.
