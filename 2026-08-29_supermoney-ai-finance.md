**Date:** 2026-08-29

## Summary
An AI personal finance app on Product Hunt that goes beyond tracking — surfaces actionable money-saving recommendations like debt optimization and better product alternatives. Positioned as an AI advisor, not just a budget tracker.

## Idea angle
The shift from tracking to advising is the key AI value prop in personal finance. Most apps show you what happened; SuperMoney tells you what to do next. Building this for the Indonesian market — where financial products are fragmented and comparison shopping is manual — would have strong product-market fit.
