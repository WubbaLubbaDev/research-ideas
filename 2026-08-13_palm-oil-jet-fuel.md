# Indonesia aims to commercialize jet fuels from palm oil in 2-3 years
**Date:** 2026-08-13
**Keyword:** palm oil
**Source:** Hacker News
**URL:** https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45925847

## Summary
Indonesia plans to commercialize aviation biofuel derived from palm oil within 2-3 years, creating a massive new demand stream for palm oil beyond food and consumer products.

## Idea angle
Build a market intelligence platform tracking the palm oil-to-biofuel supply chain — from plantation to refinery to airline. As Indonesia pushes biofuel mandates (B35→B40 biodiesel, now aviation fuel), the demand equation for palm oil shifts fundamentally. A data product tracking mandate compliance, refinery capacity, and price impacts would serve traders, producers, and policymakers.
