## Summary
Lums is an AI accounting engine on Product Hunt that lets you chat with your financial data and automatically builds a mathematically-correct budget. Launched May 6, 2026. Combines conversational AI with guaranteed-correct accounting math.

## Idea angle
Conversational finance + guaranteed-correct math is the holy grail — LLMs are great at explaining but bad at arithmetic. Lums solves this by separating the reasoning (LLM) from the calculation (deterministic engine). This architecture pattern — AI for UX, deterministic for computation — applies to any domain where accuracy is non-negotiable.
