## Summary
TradingAgents is a multi-agent LLM framework for financial trading that deploys specialized AI agents (analysts, researchers, traders, risk managers) to collaborate on investment decisions. Each agent has a distinct role mimicking a real trading firm's organizational structure.

## Idea angle
Build a multi-agent trading system for Indonesian markets (IDX) where agents specialize in different sectors (banking, commodities, property) and debate investment theses. The multi-agent debate format could surface blind spots a single-model approach misses — especially in emerging markets where sentiment and political risk dominate.
