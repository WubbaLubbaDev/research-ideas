## Summary
A browser-based stock trading simulator for Chinese A-shares. All data is generated and stored locally — no real market data or real money involved. Designed for learning trading rules and testing strategies in a risk-free environment.

## Idea angle
Build a similar simulator for Indonesian IDX stocks with realistic market mechanics (auto-rejection, circuit breakers, IDX trading hours). The gap: no good free IDX trading simulator exists for Indonesian retail investors to practice.
