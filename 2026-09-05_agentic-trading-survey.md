# Agentic Trading Survey — LLM Agents in Financial Markets
**Date:** 2026-09-05
**Keyword:** stock trading
**Source:** arXiv
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19337

## Summary
A comprehensive survey of 77 studies on LLM-based trading agents, reframing them as expert-system decision pipelines. Only 2/19 primary studies report time-consistent data splits, 1/19 specify transaction costs, and 0/19 reach R3 reproducibility — exposing severe protocol and reproducibility gaps in the field.

## Idea angle
The reproducibility crisis in agentic trading research is the real finding here. Building a standardized evaluation harness — with mandatory transaction cost models, survivorship bias controls, and time-consistent splits — would become the field's reference implementation. The survey's reporting checklist could be turned into an automated linter for trading research papers.

## Details
Authors: Yihan Xia, Panpan You, Taotao Wang, et al. (Shenzhen University) | Published in: Expert Systems with Applications | arXiv:2605.19337 | 77 studies surveyed, 19 in primary subset | Key finding: 0/19 reach R3 reproducibility
