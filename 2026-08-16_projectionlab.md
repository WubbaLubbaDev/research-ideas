## Summary
ProjectionLab is a personal finance simulator built over 2 years, allowing users to model their financial future with Monte Carlo simulations, scenario analysis, and retirement planning. It goes beyond simple budgeting to show probabilistic outcomes of financial decisions.

## Idea angle
Build a similar financial projection tool for Indonesian users with local assumptions: BPJS-THT, SBN (government bonds), IDX dividend yields, rupiah inflation, and Indonesian tax brackets. The gap: no Indonesian-specific financial simulator accounts for local retirement schemes and tax structures.
