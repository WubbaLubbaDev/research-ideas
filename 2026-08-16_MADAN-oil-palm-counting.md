## Summary
MADAN (Multi-level Attention Domain Adaptation Network) addresses cross-regional oil palm tree counting and detection. It uses domain adaptation to transfer detection models across different plantation regions and conditions, enabling accurate tree counting in new areas without retraining from scratch.

## Idea angle
Use MADAN's domain adaptation approach to scale oil palm tree counting across Indonesia's diverse plantation geographies (Sumatra vs. Kalimantan vs. Sulawesi). The gap: tree counting models trained on one region fail in others due to different terrain, planting patterns, and tree varieties. Domain adaptation could enable rapid deployment across all Indonesian plantation regions.
